# Switching Social over

Read [the shared list](README.md) first: what has to be true of the live site before any tool moves.

The risk here is simple to state: **a post must go out from one place only.** Your earlier content app hands posts to a posting service, which sends them at their time even if the app itself is closed. The suite posts straight to Facebook and Instagram itself.

## Before the day

**Connections**

- **Facebook & Instagram** (Connections, or Social → Set up → "Allow posting on Facebook"). It asks for posting on your Pages and their Instagram accounts. Then **Settings → Social & emails → Social accounts**: tick the Pages and Instagram accounts to post to and give each its brand.
- **TikTok and LinkedIn** (optional): offered only once the live site has their keys ([tiktok-linkedin.md](../tiktok-linkedin.md)). Until TikTok has reviewed the app, every TikTok video goes up as "Only you".

**Brands.** Your other sites are brands in this one workspace. Add them at Settings → Brand kit (colours and voice for each) before giving them accounts.

**Nothing comes across by file.** There is no import for the library, projects, posts, emails or the link page. Set these up by hand:

| From your earlier content app        | Here                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Photos and videos                    | Social → Assets. JPEG and PNG up to 8 MB, MP4 and MOV up to 50 MB; WebP photos need saving as JPEG first. Tag as you go. |
| Projects you're still posting from   | Social → Projects                                                                                                        |
| Topics, posting times, review day    | Social → Plan                                                                                                            |
| Brand voice and Claude's style notes | Settings → Brand kit (voice) and Settings → Social & emails → Style notes, one set per brand                             |
| Who approves posts                   | Settings → Social & emails → Post review                                                                                 |
| Link page links                      | Social → Link page (https links only)                                                                                    |
| Marketing emails you reuse           | Rebuilt in Social → Emails, or keep copying them from the earlier app                                                    |
| Posts still to go out                | See "On the day"                                                                                                         |

Then finish **Social → Set up**.

## What will be different

- **No process reels, no video editor, no avatar videos.** Make videos elsewhere and upload the finished file.
- **No results.** Likes, comments and reach aren't read back, so there is no "what's working" for drafts to learn from. Check them on each network.
- **No Pinterest.** TikTok takes videos only.
- **No tools for Claude to use from outside the app** (looking at the library, drafting posts, reading the schedule from a chat elsewhere). Use the composer's own drafting here.
- **Photos:** no photo editor (straighten, crop, light), no making a video smaller before upload, no chosen cover frame, no bringing photos in from Google Drive or Canva, no pasting from the clipboard. Product photos come from what your shop sync stored, the main photo only.
- **Writing posts:** posts are sorted by your plan's topics rather than fixed post types; no copy of a post, no team notes on a post, no per-network crop previews, no Instagram grid preview, no review checklist, no searchable list of every post, no week view or dragging posts around, no "turn this post into an email", no workshop ads tied to one workshop. Prices are typed, not read from the shop.
- **One voice per brand**, not a style per network.
- **The link page** has no picture or logo of its own, no themes, no tap counts.
- **Emails here aren't sent**: copy the finished HTML into your email tool, as before.

## On the day, in order

1. **Pick the day.** From then on, posts are made and scheduled only in the suite.
2. **In your earlier content app, switch off anything that drafts or schedules by itself** (its automatic monthly drafting).
3. **For each post the earlier app has already scheduled**, choose one of these, never both:
   - let it go out from the earlier app as planned; **or**
   - take it off the posting service in the earlier app first, check it's gone there, and only then schedule it here.
     Posts due in the next few days are easiest left where they are.
4. **Schedule your first post here** for later the same day, to one account, and watch it go.
5. Only once your earlier app has nothing left waiting to go out, consider switching the suite's monthly run on (Social → Plan; off until an owner or admin switches it on). It only ever makes drafts.

## Check it worked

**First hour**

- The test post shows as posted on Social → Calendar and is on the Page or account itself.
- Nothing appears under "Didn't post".

**First day**

- Everything that went out today went out once: look at each account's own feed, not just the calendar.
- The earlier app's list of scheduled posts is empty from the switch day on, or holds only the ones you chose to leave there.

## If something goes wrong

Go back by scheduling in your earlier content app again. Before you do, take back here whatever you've moved: open each scheduled post and press "Save as draft", so the two don't both send it.

What can't be undone: a post that has gone out. Delete it on the network itself if it shouldn't be there. A post that only partly went out (say Facebook but not Instagram) can't be edited here, but "Try the rest again" sends only to the accounts it didn't reach. If the suite never heard back about one, it asks you to look on the network and say whether it's there; it never sends it again by itself.

**To confirm:** that "Save as draft" on a scheduled post takes it off the schedule.

## Still needs a decision or isn't ready

- **The live site update** ([the shared list](README.md), item 1): accounts per brand, review, sizes, Stories and the monthly run all need it. Needs your go-ahead.
- **The live site's Meta keys**, and Meta's review of the app's posting permissions for anyone outside the app's own team. **To confirm:** whether your accounts can connect before that review (they can if you have a role on the app).
- **TikTok and LinkedIn keys** on the live site, and TikTok's review before videos can be public.
- **An import of the library and the scheduled posts** isn't built. If the library is large, that is worth deciding before the day rather than uploading by hand.
- Videos, results, Pinterest and the outside tools for Claude are not built.
