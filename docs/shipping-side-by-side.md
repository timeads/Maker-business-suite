# Shipping: old help desk app vs. the suite, side by side

Read-only audit, 23 Nov 2026. Nothing in either codebase was changed.

**How to read the file references**

- **Old** paths are inside `/home/user/timeads/helpdesk` (for example `src/routes/shipping.ts:233`).
- **New** paths are inside `/home/user/maker-business-suite`. To keep the tables narrow:
  - `ship/` means `src/app/w/[slug]/(app)/shipping/`
  - `lib/` means `src/lib/shipping/`
  - `mig/` means `supabase/migrations/`
- **Matched** means the code for it was found in the suite. **Partly** means some of it was found. **Missing** means it was looked for and not found.
- Size to close: **S** = a day or two, **M** = about a week, **L** = several weeks or a new integration.

---

## 1. Summary

The old app has **123** shipping capabilities that a person packing or running the shop would notice. In the suite, **57 are matched**, **28 are partly matched** and **38 are missing**. The suite already covers the daily basics for domestic parcels bought through EasyPost: the to-ship queue and its views, holds with end dates, ship-together, several boxes on one purchase, packing slips with a barcode, scan-to-check packing, marking orders sent in the shop, and a small postage-versus-collected report. What it does not have yet is almost everything that makes the old app fast or cheap for this business: the business's own UPS account and Redo as places to buy postage (and the side-by-side price comparison between them), international labels and customs paperwork, buying a label straight from the scan station, direct thermal (Zebra) printing, and the Shopify follow-ups for pickup and voided labels.

## 3. Must-have before switching

In priority order. "Stops the day" means packing can't go on inside the suite; "costs money" means each parcel costs more or money is at risk.

1. **The business's own UPS account and Redo as places to buy postage, shown in one price list.** _Costs money on every parcel._ The old app's whole buying screen is built around comparing three sources (own UPS, EasyPost, Redo) and keeping the cheapest (`src/lib/carriers.ts:12-65`). The suite can only buy through EasyPost. Until this exists, anything cheaper on UPS direct or Redo has to be bought outside the suite and typed in by hand, which also loses printing, the scan check and the profit figure for those parcels. Build UPS direct first if most labels go on the negotiated UPS account (see section 5: the code can't tell which is used most).
2. **International labels and customs paperwork.** _Stops the day for every order going abroad._ The suite refuses them outright (`ship/orders/[id]/page.tsx:61-62`). The old app fills the customs list from the shop, remembers each product, prints the commercial invoice and blocks shipments that need an export filing.
3. **Shipping rules, packing memory and buying labels in bulk.** _Built: see "Rules & automation", "Boxes, packing & packing memory" and "Buy labels in bulk" above._ _Stops a busy day._ These three work as one: rules and memory decide the box, weight, signature and service so that "tick twenty orders, buy" is safe. Without them every order is opened, boxed and weighed by hand, and rules that hold orders or demand a signature (a money risk on expensive parcels) aren't applied. The suite's single "signature over $X" setting covers only part of the signature case.
4. **Printing straight to the Zebra thermal printer.** _Slows every parcel._ The packing bench prints labels and 4×6 slips with no dialog today. In the suite each label is an image in a new tab that has to be printed by hand, and there is no "already printed" warning.
5. **Telling the shop about pickups and voids.** _Customer-facing mistakes._ In the suite, "ready for pickup" never reaches the shop, so the customer is never emailed to come and collect; and voiding a label leaves the order showing as shipped (with a dead tracking number already emailed) until someone fixes it in the shop by hand.
6. **Buying the label from the scan station** (verify, then print; auto-print; box-by-box for split orders). _Slows every scanned parcel._ The suite's scan page checks the items but then sends you elsewhere to buy.
7. **Shipping part of an order with a bought label, with the rest held automatically.** _Stops out-of-stock days._ The suite can only do part shipments for parcels bought elsewhere, and what's left stays in "Ready".
8. **Saved choices per order, and the "L-12" saved labels.** The old workflow sets a label up at a desk and buys it at the packing computer. In the suite the setup is lost when the page is left.
9. **Remembering address checks.** _Small cost each time._ The old app notes EasyPost charges about 2¢ per check (`README.md:130`) and checks each address once; the suite asks again on every open.
10. **Marketplace ship-by dates.** Late Amazon shipments carry penalties; the suite shows no deadline.

Can wait until after the switch: rate calculator, weekly chart and by-service table, label batches tab, slip logo and photos, more hold presets, arrow keys, email starters, Redo rate check, Redo history import (do the import once in the old app before leaving it).
