# Bookings: the current booking app vs. the suite, side by side

Read-only audit, 25 Nov 2026. Nothing in either codebase was changed.

**How to read the file references**

- **Old** paths are inside the current booking app's repository, `TuftBooking` (for example `widget/src/cart.ts:40`). `old-mig/` means its `supabase/migrations/` and `old-fn/` means its `supabase/functions/`.
- **New** paths are inside this repository. To keep the tables narrow:
  - `book/` means `src/app/w/[slug]/(app)/bookings/`
  - `lib/` means `src/lib/bookings/`
  - `mig/` means `supabase/migrations/`
- **Matched** means the code for it was found in the suite. **Partly** means some of it was found, or it could not be fully confirmed. **Missing** means it was looked for and not found.
- Size to close: **S** = a day or two, **M** = about a week, **L** = several weeks or a new outside service.
- The current app has grown well past its own `SPEC.md` (Eventbrite, Google Calendar, classroom supplies, a budget page, shared seat limits, a cart guard). The tables follow the code, not the spec.

---

## 1. Summary

The current app has **89** things a person running workshops, or a customer booking one, would notice. In the suite, **76 are matched**, **12 are partly matched** and **1 is missing**. (Counts updated 26 Nov 2026 after items 1 to 10 and 12 to 15 of section 4 were built.)

The suite already covers the heart of it: the date picker on the shop's product page (same button, same calendar, same "Session ID" on the cart line), shop orders turning into bookings once and only once, refunds and cancellations giving seats back, a paid order never being dropped, private and closed dates, weekly repeats, the day-of roster with check-in, notes, tags, moving and cancelling a person, adding someone by hand, a spreadsheet of who's coming, and the confirmation, reminder and thank-you emails.

What is not there yet is the Google Calendar that grew inside the booking app. Eventbrite as a second place to sell is now built (`docs/eventbrite.md`). Order tags in the shop, text reminders through the business's own Twilio account, classroom supplies with their vendors, and the budget are now built. Shared seat limits, the day-to-day date changes and a spreadsheet import of the current app's workshops, dates and bookings are now built.

## 3. What the suite does that the current app doesn't

- **Its own booking pages with card payment.** A workshop that isn't sold through the shop can be booked and paid for on the suite's `/book` page, the money going straight to the business's own Stripe account (`src/app/book`, `lib/server.ts`, `lib/payments.ts`).
- **Appointments.** One-to-one times offered from weekly open hours, which never overlap a workshop (`lib/slots.ts`, `tests/db/bookings.test.ts:263`, `:376`).
- **Seats held while someone pays** on the suite's own pages, and let go if they walk away (`tests/db/bookings.test.ts:166-240`).
- **A link for the customer to cancel or move their own booking**, in every confirmation (`src/app/booking/[token]/page.tsx`, `mig/20261126000100_bookings.sql:485-546`).
- **A waitlist**: people waiting are emailed when a place frees up (`src/app/api/book/waitlist/route.ts`, `tests/db/bookings.test.ts:323`).
- **Your own questions on the booking form**, with the answers on the roster and in the spreadsheet (`lib/config.ts`, `book/sessions/[id]/roster/route.ts`).
- **Cancelling a date emails everyone on it** (`book/actions.ts:184`).
- **Check-in and notes open to the whole team**, while scheduling, moving and cancelling stay with managers (`tests/db/bookings.test.ts:497`).
- **A tick to go over the limit** when adding someone by hand, rather than raising the limit first.
- **Orders that are edited later are followed**, because the suite reads what the order holds now each time the shop mentions it.
- **Heads-ups in Messages** when an order names a date that can't be found or has been cancelled (`lib/shop.ts:58-75`).
- **A record of every message sent to a whole date** (`booking_messages`).
- **Built for more than one business**, with each business's bookings walled off from the others (`tests/db/bookings.test.ts:90-160`).
