# Social: the current content app vs. the suite, side by side

Read-only audit, 26 Nov 2026. Nothing in either codebase was changed. Updated the same day after build items 1, 2 and 6 (review and approve, slides kept on the post, Instagram Stories and photo sizing), and again after the brands decision of item 7 was built: several brands in one workspace, each with its own kit and its own social accounts, and several Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts on one login; and again after TikTok and LinkedIn were added (build item 11), each posted to directly through our own app; and again after the monthly run (item 4) and learning from edits (item 5) were built; and again after the rest of item 4 and most of item 7 (slides written by Claude, shot order and photo marks, drafts from a project, writing a draft again).

**How to read the file references**

- **Old** paths are inside the current content app's repository, `stitch-post` (the "Content Hub"). `old-pages/` means its `src/pages/`, `old-fn/` means its `supabase/functions/`, `old-mig/` means its `supabase/migrations/` and `old-mcp/` means its `src/lib/mcp/tools/` (the tools Claude can use).
- **New** paths are inside this repository. To keep the tables narrow:
  - `soc/` means `src/app/w/[slug]/(app)/social/`
  - `lib/` means `src/lib/social/`
  - `mig/` means `supabase/migrations/`
- **Matched** means the code for it was found in the suite. **Partly** means some of it was found, or it could not be fully confirmed. **Missing** means it was looked for and not found.
- Size to close: **S** = a day or two, **M** = about a week, **L** = several weeks or a new outside service.
- The current app's README and How-to page describe an early version (Publer, weekly "content plans"). The tables follow the code as it is now: posting goes through a paid go-between service (Zernio), and the month is drafted around a review day.

## 1. Summary

The current app has **102** things a person making and posting content would notice. In the suite, **48 are matched**, **23 are partly matched** and **31 are missing**.

The suite already covers the everyday core: a photo and video library with tags, folders and search; projects that gather one piece of work's photos and posts; posts with up to ten photos in order or one video, a caption of their own for Facebook or Instagram where the words differ, and a caption Claude drafts for a person to edit; the slide designer with the brand layouts; a month calendar; scheduling; posting straight to a Facebook Page and its Instagram account (photos, swipe-through posts and Reels), never twice, with failures shown, retried and emailed to the owners; a content plan of topics and posting times; "Draft the month"; the email builder; and a link page.

Since then the suite has gained the review-and-approve step (with "approve all"), carousels kept as one design on the post until approval, Instagram Stories, photos sized for Instagram as they are sent, and brands: a workspace keeps several (each with its own colours and voice), and one Facebook login can hold several Pages, each with its linked Instagram account, each used as one of the brands.

Since then the suite has also gained the monthly run (a review day per brand, the month drafted by itself for review, carousels from each project's unposted photos with Claude looking at them) and learning from edits (what Claude wrote kept beside what people changed it to, style notes per brand that Claude can suggest from those edits and a person saves, and both handed to every caption draft).

Since then the suite has also gained the rest of the monthly run and projects: slides written by Claude (from the composer, "Draft the month" and the monthly run, for topics made as slides), photos in the order they were taken and marked finished, process or artwork, "Draft from this project", "Write it again" on a draft Claude wrote (the earlier version kept), and what Claude saw in a project's photos kept and handed to its next drafts.

What is not there yet: facts read from the other sites for their series posts, the tools Claude uses from outside the app, process reels and the video editor, results from the networks, and Pinterest.

## 3. What the suite does that the current app doesn't

- **Posts go straight to Facebook and Instagram**, with no go-between service to pay or to wait on for "did it post?" (`lib/publish.ts`).
- **Never posted twice.** Each network's answer is kept as it comes; a post that went out on one network and not the other only tries the one that didn't (`finish_social_post`, `tests/db/social.test.ts`).
- **Failures look after themselves first**: two more tries on its own, then "Didn't post", an email to the owners and a count on the home screen (`lib/worker.ts`, `src/lib/attention.ts`).
- **A post that is partly or fully out can't be changed by accident** (`save_social_post`).
- **Uploads are checked for what they really are** once they reach storage, not for what the browser says (`finishUpload`).
- **Put away rather than delete**: a file can be hidden from the library without breaking a post that used it (`social_assets.hidden`).
- **Product photos arrive from the shop sync**, each copied once and tagged with its name, ready for posts and emails (`importProductPhotos`).
- **Photos suggested by their tags, with the reason shown** on the draft ("matched your tag: …") (`lib/photo-match.ts`).
- **A brand kit** a workspace can set up from five answers or an uploaded guide, which the designer, the emails and Claude all follow (`src/lib/brand/`).
- **The link page can show the latest posted photos** under the links (`links-server.ts`).
- **The assistant can suggest changes** to the content plan and the link page for an owner to look at and apply, with every change kept and reversible (`src/lib/assistant/registry.ts`).
- **The same Facebook connection runs the help desk's Instagram and Facebook messages and comments** (`docs/meta-messages.md`).
- **A whole team**, each person signed in as themselves, and **more than one business**, each walled off from the others (`tests/db/social.test.ts`).
- **Workshops come from Bookings**, so dates, places and sign-up links are never typed twice.

### Things to settle before the switch

- **One place posts from, at a time.** Pick a day; from then on schedule only in the suite.
- **Which brands live where.** Decided: brands inside one workspace. Set the other two sites up as brands and give each its accounts before their posts come across; each imported post then names its brand and accounts.
- **Instagram sizes.** Built (item 6): photos that don't fit are sent as cropped copies when a post is scheduled or approved in the suite. Posts brought across as drafts get their copies when someone schedules them.
- **An import that can be run twice.** Like the suite's other imports, it should offer a look first and change nothing when run again with the same files.
