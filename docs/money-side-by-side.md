# Money: the current finance dashboard vs. the suite, side by side

Read-only audit, 26 Nov 2026. Nothing in either codebase was changed. Updated as "what to build next" item 1 (business lines), items 4 and 5 (expense sorting, profit by line), and then items 2 and 3 in part (fuller QuickBooks, cash health), 7 (budget and break-even), 9 (the cost cutter) and 6 (debt payoff and the emergency fund) were built: the rows marked as such below.

**How to read the file references**

- **Old** paths are inside the current finance dashboard's repository, `ttw-dashboard` ("TTW Finance"). `old-pages/` means its `src/pages/`, `old-hooks/` means its `src/hooks/`, `old-comp/` means its `src/components/`, `old-lib/` means its `src/lib/`, `old-fn/` means its `supabase/functions/` and `old-mig/` means its `supabase/migrations/`.
- **New** paths are inside this repository. To keep the tables narrow:
  - `money/` means `src/app/w/[slug]/(app)/dashboard/`
  - `lib/` means `src/lib/dashboard/`
  - `conn/` means `src/lib/connections/providers/`
  - `mig/` means `supabase/migrations/`
- **Matched** means the code for it was found in the suite. **Partly** means some of it was found, or it could not be fully confirmed. **Missing** means it was looked for and not found.
- Size to close: **S** = a day or two, **M** = about a week, **L** = several weeks or a new outside service.
- The current app has grown well past its own README (which is the first product brief, with a Transactions page, a Debt page, a P&L page and Settings). It now also has Budget, Cost Cutter, Profitability with an expense inbox, a Financial Advisor page, a 3-year projection, QuickBooks invoices, a time tracker and a feed for the Tuft Life hub. The tables follow the code, not the brief.
- No figures from the business are copied here. Where the current app hard-codes an amount or a percentage, the tables say so without repeating it.

## 1. Summary

The current app has **106** things a person checking the business's money would notice, counting its calculation rules. In the suite, **56 are matched**, **30 are partly matched** and **20 are missing**.

The suite's Money tool is a small, sound start: one page of widgets (key figures, sales over time, gross margin, best sellers, sales by channel, cash on hand, what customers owe), one date range for the whole page in the workspace's own timezone and currency, layouts kept as validated data, Shopify orders kept fresh by webhooks and an hourly look, and QuickBooks' profit and loss, balance sheet and aged receivables read every six hours.

Since the audit the suite has gained the business's own lines, expense sorting, profit by line, cash health (runway, burn, growth, operating profit and the advisor's tiles and notes, from the cash flow statement and three years of books), month, quarter and year ranges, a budget with break-even, the cost cutter, and the debt payoff plan with its emergency fund, debt tiles, budget rows and what-if (for owners and admins only). What the current app still has and the suite does not: the full QuickBooks statements as pages, invoices, benchmarks, a 3-year projection with a PDF, shipping margin by month, the AI summaries and checks, and a time tracker.

**Sorting by account (added after the owner tried it on real books).** The account is now the unit of sorting: Settings → Money → Sorting expenses opens with every expense account the books use (count and total over the last 12 months) and one choice per account: a line, a split, shared, set aside, or "decide each one". Order of decision: a person's own choice, firm rules, payee defaults, other rules, then the account (`decideExpense`). An account with no choice yet counts as **shared**, so nothing waits in the inbox unless its account is set to "decide each one" (or a person put it back, or a rule only suggests). The current app has nothing like this: it sorted one transaction at a time. QuickBooks' own Class and Location tracking is now kept on each expense (read from the answers the sync already asks for, no new question) and rules can match a class or a location; the current app doesn't read either.
